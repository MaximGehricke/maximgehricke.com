Emirates ICE (In-Cabin-Entertainment) - Lighting and FX (2025)

The Emirates ICE project I got to work on in 2025 was one of the most fun and diverse experiences at Framestore.

I worked on the lighting for four different shots and built a Matrix-inspired shelf setup. With some remaining time, I also added extra details to the plane asset and created subtle FX layers for the turbines using Pyro and Particles.

Wheel of Time Season III - FX (2024)

Wheel of Time Season III was one of the longest and certainly the largest project of my career.

At Framestore we were responsible for the channeling magic FX. I was mostly working with a quite extensive, very art-directable setup to do shotwork on those FX, but also got to do some blood/gore as well as build an FX setup from scratch for the Balefire Beam.

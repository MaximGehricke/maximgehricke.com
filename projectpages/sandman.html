Sandman Season II (Matthew the Raven) - Lighting (2025)

In 2025 I got to work a relatively short lighting stint on Sandman Season II. We were mostly working on the talking raven "Matthew".

When I joined, the project was already well underway, so for most of my shots I simply maintained the momentum by updating anim/cfx caches and making small lighting tweaks.

However, I did get the chance to set up a few lighting rigs from scratch on two different shots, stitching HDRIs and creating lighting plates and techgrades.

COX mobile - Annie The Sheep Creature FX (Framestore 2024)

In 2023, I briefly worked on a COX Mobile ad, managing the CFX for "Annie the sheep" in a shot designed for TikTok and social media.

I had gained some CFX experience during the previous months on the "Avatar: Last Airbender" project, and I was able to apply that knowledge to a different CFX rig.

The shot wasn't simple, as it was quite lengthy and required careful attention to detail, especially with Annie's glow stick rings, which needed to collide realistically.

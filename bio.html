Maxim Gehricke

FX TD / Generalist TD / DIRECTOR


I am deeply technical with strong hands-on expertise in Houdini, VEX, Maya and Nuke.

I particularly enjoy automating processes using Python and building tools in Houdini that speed up workflows for the team.

Outside of my dayjob in VFX I am looking to break ground as a director and cinematographer with my 3D animated shortfilm "SEN (2026)". This shortfilm, predominantly created using Maya and Arnold, was a passion-project that I have spent more than 5 years building.

If you are looking for a driven technical artist with a broad skillset and FX focus, please don't hesitate to reach out.
